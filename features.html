Features

Everything you need for everyday healthcare

Explore a range of useful healthcare features designed to make healthcare information more accessible and convenient.

Comprehensive Medicine Information

Explore detailed information about medicines available in Bangladesh, including uses, indications, dosage, administration, side effects, contraindications, precautions, interactions, and prices.

Disease Information

Learn about common diseases, their symptoms, treatment information, and preventive measures through accessible healthcare information.

Doctor Selector

Find guidance on selecting appropriate medical specialists based on your symptoms and healthcare needs.

Doctor & Chamber Directory

Explore doctor information and chamber details to help identify healthcare professionals and their consultation locations.

Hospital & Healthcare Directory

Discover healthcare facilities, including hospitals, pharmacies, ambulance services, and other essential healthcare resources.

Medicine Reminder

Create medication reminders to help you remember scheduled medicines and maintain your medication routine.

Medicine Cost Calculator

Estimate medicine expenses and calculate the cost of medication requirements conveniently.

Health Tips

Access informative healthcare tips about healthy living, disease prevention, and everyday health practices.

First Aid Guidance

Explore basic first-aid information to understand responses to common medical emergencies and situations.

Medical Calculators

Use practical healthcare calculators, including Body Mass Index (BMI), Ideal Body Weight (IBW), and Estimated Delivery Date (EDD).

Male & Female Weight Charts

Explore separate weight charts for males and females to understand and monitor weight-related health information.

My Medicine

Organize and bookmark medicine lists for yourself and family members. Share your medicine lists conveniently.

Search & Favorites

Save frequently accessed medicines to your favorites for convenient future reference.

Plus Patient Aid Premium

Ad-free experience, multiple profiles in My Medicine, unlimited Bangla medicine details, cloud bookmarks, daily medicine data updates, and online backup.

Offline & bilingual

Healthcare information that travels with you

Two things make Patient Aid useful in everyday situations: it works without a constant connection, and it speaks your language.

Available medicine and healthcare information can be accessed offline, while Bangla and English support means information stays understandable for the whole family.

  • Read healthcare information in Bangla and English
