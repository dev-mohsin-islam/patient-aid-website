1. Overview

Patient Aid is a healthcare information application developed by ITmedicus Solutions for the people of Bangladesh. This page explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you use the application, and how you can contact us about privacy.

This website is an informational marketing site. It does not include accounts, search tools, bookings, or other dynamic features, and it does not collect information through web forms.

2. Information the app may handle

Patient Aid is designed to work largely with healthcare reference information stored on your device. Some features may involve information you choose to enter or save, such as:

  • Medicine lists and bookmarks you create in My Medicine.
  • Medication reminders and the schedules you set.
  • Profiles you create for family members (available with Premium).
  • Language and display preferences.

The final policy should state precisely which of these items are stored only on the device and which are synchronized to a server when online backup or cloud bookmarks are enabled.

3. How information is used

Information saved in the app is intended to provide the features you use — for example, displaying your medicine list, showing reminders, or restoring a backup. The final policy should confirm whether any information is used for analytics, diagnostics, or service improvement, and describe the legal basis for that processing.

4. Online features and backups

Some Patient Aid features require an internet connection, including synchronization, online backup for My Medicine, cloud bookmarks, and medicine data updates. When these features are used, relevant information may be transmitted to and stored on servers operated for Patient Aid.

The final policy should describe where that data is stored, how long it is retained, how it is protected, and how you can request deletion.

5. Third-party services

The application is distributed through Google Play, and Premium subscriptions are handled through Google Play's billing system. Google's own privacy policy applies to information processed by Google Play. The final policy should list any other third-party services used by the application, such as analytics or crash-reporting providers.

6. Your choices

  • You can remove medicines, lists, and reminders from within the application.
  • You can choose whether to enable online backup or cloud features.
  • You can contact us to ask questions about information associated with your use of the app.

7. Children's privacy

Patient Aid is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. The final policy should state the applicable age requirements and how information relating to children is treated.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application changes or when legal requirements change. The current version should always be available at the official policy URL, with a clear "last updated" date.
