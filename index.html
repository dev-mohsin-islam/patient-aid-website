About Patient Aid

Healthcare information at your fingertips

Making reliable healthcare information accessible to everyone is at the heart of Patient Aid.

Developed by ITmedicus Solutions, Patient Aid brings together comprehensive medicine information, disease information, health tips, first-aid guidance, healthcare directories, and practical medication management features in a single application.

Designed especially for the Bangladeshi community, Patient Aid helps users access essential healthcare information in both Bangla and English, even without a continuous internet connection.

More about Patient Aid

Inside the app

  • Medicine information with brand and generic names
  • Disease information, symptoms, and prevention
  • Health tips and first-aid guidance
  • Doctor, chamber, hospital, and ambulance directories
  • Medicine reminders and cost estimation
  • My Medicine lists for you and your family

Patient Aid is an information companion. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Offline healthcare

Healthcare information, even without the internet

Not everyone has access to a reliable internet connection all the time. That's why Patient Aid is designed with offline accessibility in mind.

Access essential healthcare and medicine information directly from your device without requiring a continuous internet connection.

  • Access available offline medicine information
  • Explore previously synchronized healthcare information
  • Get essential information when connectivity is unavailable
  • Enjoy convenient access while travelling or in areas with limited connectivity

Some features, online services, synchronization, and updated information may require an internet connection.
