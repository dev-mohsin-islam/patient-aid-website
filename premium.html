What Premium adds

Each Premium benefit is designed around everyday use — fewer interruptions, more family profiles, and better backup.

Ad-free experience

Multiple profiles in My Medicine

Good to know

Premium availability, subscription conditions, and feature access are subject to the terms displayed within the application.

  • Where to subscribe: Premium options are presented inside the Patient Aid application.
  • Internet connection: Cloud bookmarks, online backup, and data updates require an internet connection.
  • Free features: Patient Aid remains free to download, and many features are available without Premium.
  • Questions: Contact ITmedicus Solutions if you need help with your subscription.

The website does not process payments or manage subscriptions. All subscription activity happens inside the app or through Google Play.
