About Patient Aid

Healthcare information at your fingertips

Making reliable healthcare information accessible to everyone is at the heart of Patient Aid.

Patient Aid is a comprehensive offline healthcare information application designed for the people of Bangladesh. Developed by ITmedicus Solutions, it brings together comprehensive medicine information, disease information, health tips, first-aid guidance, healthcare directories, and practical medication management features in a single application.

Designed especially for the Bangladeshi community, Patient Aid helps users access essential healthcare information in both Bangla and English, even without a continuous internet connection. Whether you want to learn about a medicine, understand a health condition, find healthcare facilities, or organize your family's medicine lists, Patient Aid makes it easier.

Purpose

Healthcare information is not always easy to find, and it is not always available when people need it most. Patient Aid exists to close that gap — putting clear, organized healthcare information in the hands of patients and families, and keeping it accessible when connectivity is limited.

Who it is for

  • Patients and families looking for understandable medicine and disease information.
  • Caregivers managing medicines for more than one person.
  • General users who want reliable health resources in Bangla or English.

An information companion, not a replacement

Patient Aid provides healthcare and medicine information for general educational and informational purposes. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. All medicine, doctor, hospital, and medication-management functionality stays inside the Android application.
